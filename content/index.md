# Caged Squirrels

<div class="placeholder" markdown="1">
<p>Long overdue. 30 years ago I wrote a game called crossfir.com . If you have to ask why I dropped the 'e', then you were not born yet. But that's how long I have not managed to find time to fullfil a dream.
Recently discovered I have struggled through many episodes of life, with ADHD. So I vowed to overcome a procrastination that has haunted me all this time.</p>
<p>In typical hell-for-leather stype I whipped up a proof of concept and have thrown so many components into it, all that remains is the actual kitchen sink. Coffee is a prototype and entirely avoids the actual script. 
The prototype keeps the game script completely secret while letting me learn how to implement the mechanics and how to create an installer, and publish a free game. I hope people will play this prototype and tell me whether it runs on their machines. when it comes to the actual game I hope to have a stable engine. 
Yes I'm using Arcade and Python, but the engine is entirely mine.</p>
