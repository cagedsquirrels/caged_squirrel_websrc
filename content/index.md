# Caged Squirrels

<div class="placeholder" markdown="1">
<p>Long overdue. 30 years ago I wrote a game called crossfir.com . If you have to ask why I dropped the 'e', then you were not born yet. But that's how long I have not managed to find time to fullfil a dream.
Recently discovered I have struggled through many episodes of life, with ADHD. So I vowed to overcome a procrastination that has haunted me all this time.</p>
<p>In typical hell-for-leather stype I have whipped up a proof of concept and have thrown so many components into it, 
all that remains is the actual kitchen sink. Coffee is a prototype and entirely hides the actual script I have been dreaming of for a year, and finally have written down now.
The prototype keeps the game script completely secret while letting me learn how to write and publish a game. 
I hope people will play this prototype and tell me whether it runs on their machines. 
When it comes to the actual game I hope to have a stable engine and a smooth release, can you tell I know a bit abotu Software quality? No secret, I am a Software QA in my day job, read more about that in the devblog once I add it. 
 I'm using Arcade and Python, but the engine is entirely fresh using AI tooling and my experience. Yes the graphics are mine or paid for, so support me so I can support others.</p>
