# 01 First, why Caged Squirrels?

<p>An origin story.</p>
<p>My first game was probably <b>Space Invaders</b>, written in <b>Pascal</b>. Back when it was Borland, and it would have been DOS 3.1 . 
We used to sneak into the computer lab and steal computer tim in the back and instead of doing assignments a few of us would mess about. 
Space invaders was dead slow and it had no game engine to speak of. I think the best waty to learn to program is by writting a game, or writting an app that does a thing you love if games are not your thing.<p>
<p>My second game was written after failing to get Pascal to produce a performant game. 
I actually originally started learning programming in <b>Modula 2</b> and  'C', probably was 1985/6. And I managed to rip the INT10 BIOS routines code using a debugger which allowed a few of us to write basic graphics routines. But when you have a lot to get onto the screen, you have to give yourself an easier target.</p>

<p>So after that failure wrote a game called <b>crossfir.com</b> . If you have to ask why I dropped the 'e', then you were not born yet. 
It was a dastardly hard text mode game using extended ASCII characters. Sadly I lost all that code. </p><p>I did later write a app, it's still up on github, because Codeproject where I put it got taken down a long time ago now.</p>

<p>All that time, I have not managed to find time to fullfil a dream. Recently discovered I have struggled through many episodes of life, with ADHD. So I vowed to overcome a procrastination that has haunted me all this time.
I might never get an official diagnosis, but that matters not, because I have not got enough experience and time to write a game. Finally! 
In typical ADHD hell-for-leather style I now whipped up a proof of concept and have thrown so many components into it,
 all that remains is the actual kitchen sink. Coffee is a prototype and entirely avoids the actual inspiration behind my wanting to write games again. 
The Coffee prototype is a throwaway playable. It's a way to beta test the engine which I am writting with the help of AI. 
The coding is half me and half Opus 5.5, probably less me really. 
All the graphics are ones I have gotten free or paid for. Which is mad considering they are for a prototype, 
but I also love to sit for ours on end and tweak pixels, and so I will. I will be using loops I have bought over the years while dreaming of this day, but most of the sound effects are me bending it all in Audacity, Not Audacity 4 mind you.
Many of the specialised graphics are either generic CC commons photos I pixelated, or photos I took myself. 
Coffee still needs mroe work to be complete, it's suppsoed to be an hour of play and puzzle in a visual novel pixel art style, which matches my vision for Caged Squirrels which will come later. 
I already have a prologue planned, which will also be a free game - because the goal is obviously to make the game pay for itself, all the hosting and tools and paying of creators for their graphics will not be a labour of love alone.
Coffee keeps the real game script completely secret, while letting me learn how to write and publish a game in 2026. I hope people will play this first prototype and tell me whether it runs on their machines. </p>

<p>I hope than when it comes to the Final Caged Squirrel story, I hope to have a stable engine. 
Yes I'm using Arcade and Python, but the engine is entirely mine with my helper Claude.</p>
